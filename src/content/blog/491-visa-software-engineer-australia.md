Australia is a hot destination for software engineers due to its high standard of living, competitive salaries, and beautiful geography. 

While the Subclass 189 (Skilled Independent) and Subclass 190 (Skilled Nominated) permanent residency visas are highly competitive, the **Subclass 491 (Skilled Work Regional) visa** is an excellent pathway that is often overlooked by developers.

Here is a comprehensive guide to how the 491 visa works specifically for software engineers.

---

## What is the Subclass 491 Visa?

The Subclass 491 visa is a **provisional state-nominated points visa** that allows skilled workers to live, work, and study in designated regional areas of Australia for up to 5 years. 

### Why is it beneficial for Software Engineers?
1. **Lower Points Thresholds:** Regional states receive fewer applications, so they nominates candidates with lower points scores than in metropolitan Sydney or Melbourne.
2. **Path to Permanent Residency (PR):** After living and working in a regional area for 3 years, you can transition to permanent residency via the Subclass 191 visa.
3. **State nomination boost:** Getting nominated by a state automatically adds **15 points** to your migration points calculation.

---

## Migration Points System for Software Engineers

To apply, you must submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) and score a minimum of 65 points (which includes the state nomination points). Points are awarded for:

| Factor | Points Criteria | Max Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Age** | 25 to 32 years | 30 |
| **English** | Superior (IELTS 8 / PTE 79+) | 20 |
| **Education** | Bachelor's or PhD degree | 15 |
| **Overseas Work** | 3 to 5 years of experience | 5 to 10 |
| **Nomination** | Subclass 491 Nomination | 15 |

---

## Step-by-Step Application Process

### Step 1: ACS Skills Assessment
Before applying, your software engineering qualifications and work experience must be verified by the **ACS (Australian Computer Society)**.
*   You must submit your university degree transcripts and reference letters detailing your technical duties.
*   ACS requires at least 2 years of relevant post-graduate work experience to verify your skills.

### Step 2: Language Proficiency Exam
Sit for a recognized exam like the PTE (Pearson Test of English) or IELTS. Aiming for "Superior" English (PTE 79+) is critical to maximize your points score.

### Step 3: File an Expression of Interest (EOI)
Register on the SkillSelect platform and select the Subclass 491 visa path. State nominations will review your application and send invitations to apply.

> [!WARNING]
> Ensure all reference letters and roles map closely to Australia's ANZSCO code descriptions (e.g. 261313 Software Engineer or 261312 Developer Programmer) to avoid application rejection.
